Discover Nature

Nature is full of beautiful forests, rivers, mountains, and wildlife. Exploring nature helps us understand the importance of protecting our environment and preserving natural resources.

Spending time outdoors improves physical and mental health. Activities such as hiking, bird watching, and camping allow people to enjoy the beauty of nature while learning about different plants and animals.

Quick Facts

  • Birds help spread seeds.
  • Earth has millions of species.
  • Recycling protects nature.
